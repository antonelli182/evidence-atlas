How Evidence Atlas places footage without GPS

  1. 1
    Search the archiveVAST VSS finds clips by meaning. Its location filter only knows "Toronto", which is not a place you can put a pin on.
  2. 2
    Second lookCosmos Reason re-watches each 5-second clip and reads street signs, business names and venues, and says whether people are riding or parking bicycles.
  3. 3
    OpenStreetMap matchTwo corner street signs that meet give a verified intersection. A unique named place gives a landmark. Overhead guide signs only point toward streets, so they give an area, never a pin. Coordinates always come from OpenStreetMap, never from a model.
  4. 4
    Route checkClips come from continuous drives. A match that would need the car to jump kilometres in seconds is rejected, and the reason is shown. Seeing the same sign twice does not count as support.
  5. 5
    You decideAccept or reject places, save clips, and generate a scouting card that keeps the evidence and the limits attached.
Stock phrases ("FOR LEASE", "DENTAL CLINIC") are never landmarks. Names with several OpenStreetMap matches, and street pairs that cross in two places, are treated as ambiguous. Search results are top-k, not a census: counts are clips, not people.
